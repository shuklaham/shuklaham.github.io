// about

I work on applied AI research at Nordstrom, where I build generative AI systems and multimodal AI platforms: enterprise RAG systems, vision-language models, and recommendation engines that serve 33+ million customers. My work sits at the intersection of applied ML research and large-scale production systems.

I have an M.S. in Computing & Information Science (AI specialization) from Cornell University. Before Nordstrom, I was at Amazon, where I built product classification models and ML infrastructure for retail, and at HealthTap, where I developed deep learning systems for healthcare, including answer quality prediction for a network of 90,000+ doctors.

I review for workshops at NeurIPS, ACL, EMNLP, ICLR, and COLM, and have been a recurring contributor to the AI4 conference series since 2023, first as a panelist, now as a moderator leading sessions on enterprise AI. I am interested in generative AI evaluation, vision-language models, retrieval-augmented generation, and the challenge of deploying research-grade models in production.

Broadly, I am drawn to questions about how AI systems behave in the real world: how we evaluate them rigorously, deploy them responsibly, and understand their impact at scale. My work spans an interdisciplinary arc from quantitative finance and clinical NLP to large-scale retail AI, and I believe this cross-domain perspective is essential for building AI systems that are both useful and trustworthy.
